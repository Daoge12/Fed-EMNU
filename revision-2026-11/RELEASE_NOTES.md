## Known scientific limits

The underlying noise/impair–repair principles predate this work. Fixed-budget
and capped-runtime comparisons do not certify deletion or establish universally
optimal schedules. One loss-based attack does not exhaust privacy threats.
Requester-only performance and arbitrary request streams remain untested;
PAR full-paper verification and original conflicting-result provenance remain
unresolved. Small seed counts and the simulated setting limit extrapolation.
