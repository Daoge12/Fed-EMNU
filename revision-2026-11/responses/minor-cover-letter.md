Cover Letter for Revised Fed EMNU Manuscript

22 November 2026

Dear Editor,

Please consider the minor revision of our manuscript, “Fed-EMNU: Federated Error-Maximizing Noise for Approximate Class Suppression”, by Jiale Wang, Zixiao Liu and Jianhua Wang, for publication in Software. We thank you and the reviewer for the positive assessment of the revised work and the four remaining suggestions.

The abstract and conclusion now consistently describe an implementation-oriented federated adaptation and empirical characterization, without claiming superiority over UNSIR or PAR. We have clarified the distinction between matched update budgets, common maximum wall-clock caps and actual execution times throughout the manuscript.

We have completed the public reproduction records and documented environment setup, datasets, dependency-aware replay commands and numerical summary generation. Release integrity and coverage checks, eight protocol tests and a small pipeline check passed. The full GPU matrix was not rerun for this editorial revision. We enlarged appendix table text, split wide tables and standardized notation and terminology; the reported scientific values and limitations are retained.

The accompanying reply includes a Summary of Changes, a reply to the editor and responses to all four current comments with manuscript excerpts. The clean manuscript, a highlighted comparison with the preceding revision and LaTeX source are included. The public revision branch contains the documented code, configurations and numerical metrics; raw predictions/logs, datasets and weights remain excluded, with regeneration instructions supplied.

Thank you for considering the revised manuscript.
